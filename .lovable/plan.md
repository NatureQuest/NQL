

# NatureQuest — Luxury Nature Tour Website

## Overview
A premium, cinematic single-page website for NatureQuest, a luxury nature tour agency. The site will feel like a high-end eco-resort brand with immersive visuals, elegant typography, and high-converting sections.

## Design System
- **Colors**: Deep Emerald (#0F3D3E), Midnight Black (#0B0F14), Sand Beige (#E6D5B8), Warm White (#F8F6F2), Soft Gold (#C6A75E)
- **Typography**: Playfair Display for headlines, Inter for body text, uppercase letter-spaced button text
- **Style**: Cinematic, spacious, dark luxury theme with light content breaks

## Homepage Sections (in order)

### 1. Sticky Navigation Bar
Transparent on top, turns solid dark on scroll. Logo left, nav links center, gold "Reserve" CTA button right.

### 2. Full-Screen Hero
Large background image with dark overlay. Headline: "Where Luxury Meets Wild Nature." Two CTA buttons and trust badges (5-Star Rated, Licensed & Insured, Small Private Groups, Premium Equipment).

### 3. Tour Experience Cards
6 premium cards in a responsive grid — Mangrove Kayak, Mangrove Boat, Jet Ski, Island Hopping, ATV Jungle, Round Island. Each with image, headline, description, and gold "Book Now" button. Hover zoom + overlay effect.

### 4. About NatureQuest
Elegant split layout (image + text). Premium brand story highlighting private experiences, personalized service, professional guides, and safety standards. CTA button.

### 5. Why Choose NatureQuest
Icon grid with 6 trust-building features: Private Groups, Safety Certified, Premium Equipment, Seamless Booking, Transparent Pricing, Concierge Support.

### 6. Experience the Difference
Dark cinematic section with emotional copy about the sensory experiences — paddling through mangroves, riding open waters, exploring hidden islands, conquering jungle trails.

### 7. Testimonials
Elegant slider with 5-star ratings, traveler photos, and premium quotes about safety, professionalism, and unforgettable moments.

### 8. Booking Section
High-converting form with tour selection dropdown, date picker, guest count, WhatsApp quick contact button, and urgency messaging ("Limited daily slots available"). Gold primary CTA.

### 9. Safety & Credentials
Trust-building section highlighting licensing, insurance, experienced guides, and premium maintained equipment.

### 10. Footer
Dark luxury footer with contact info, WhatsApp link, social media icons, tour quick links, privacy policy, and copyright.

### 11. Floating WhatsApp Button
Fixed bottom-right WhatsApp contact button visible on all pages.

## Key Interactions
- Smooth scroll navigation between sections
- Parallax-style effects on hero and cinematic sections
- Scroll-triggered fade-in animations throughout
- Hover effects on tour cards and buttons
- Sticky navbar with transparency transition
- Responsive mobile-first design with hamburger menu

## Technical Notes
- Single-page application with smooth scroll anchors
- No backend needed — booking form will link to WhatsApp or show a confirmation toast
- Google Fonts for Playfair Display and Inter
- All content is static/hardcoded with placeholder images
